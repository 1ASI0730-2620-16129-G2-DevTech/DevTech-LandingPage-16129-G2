Terms and Conditions of Service

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. About WashTrack and these terms

WashTrack is a digital platform developed by DevTech, a team of Software Engineering students at the Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas (UPC), as part of an academic project. WashTrack connects laundry businesses with their customers to manage orders, track garments, coordinate pickups and deliveries, process payments, and monitor washers through IoT sensors.

These Terms and Conditions apply to the WashTrack landing page and web application. By using them, you accept these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2. Accounts and roles

  • Laundry administrator: the owner or staff of a laundry who manages customers, orders, services, payments, and washers.
  • Customer: the person who requests a laundry service and tracks the status of their order.

You must provide truthful information, keep your credentials confidential, and notify us of any unauthorized use of your account. Users under 18 must have the consent of a parent or guardian.

3. Plans, subscriptions, and payments

  • Laundries can choose a Free plan (S/ 0), a Basic plan (S/ 49 per month), or a Premium plan (S/ 99 per month). Prices are expressed in Peruvian soles (PEN).
  • Customers do not pay a subscription to use WashTrack. They pay the laundry for the laundry services they request.
  • Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the paid period. There are no hidden charges.
  • Any price change will be announced at least 30 days in advance.
  • Card payments are processed by third-party payment providers. WashTrack does not store full card data.

4. Responsibilities of laundries

Each laundry is responsible for the laundry service it provides, for publishing accurate prices and estimated times, for updating the status of each order truthfully, and for the care of the garments it receives. WashTrack is a management tool and does not provide laundry services directly.

5. Responsibilities of customers

Customers must describe their garments and special care needs accurately, provide a valid address for pickups and deliveries, and pay for the services they request.

6. IoT monitoring

Data from the washer sensors (usage cycles, operating status, and alerts) is used only to generate maintenance alerts and operational metrics for the laundry. Predictions are estimates to support decisions; they do not replace the technical maintenance recommended by the manufacturer.

7. Acceptable use

You must not use WashTrack for unlawful purposes, try to access other users' accounts or data, upload malicious code, interfere with the operation of the service, or publish false or offensive information.

8. Availability and changes to the service

We work to keep WashTrack available and secure, but there may be interruptions due to maintenance or causes beyond our control. As an academic project, features may change. We will announce relevant changes to these terms on this page.

9. Intellectual property

The WashTrack name, logo, design, and source code belong to DevTech. Laundries and customers keep ownership of the information they register on the platform.

10. Limitation of liability

DevTech is not responsible for damages caused by misuse of the platform, by the services of third parties (payment providers, hosting, or embedded videos), or by force majeure. Nothing in these terms limits the rights recognized by the Consumer Protection and Defense Code of Peru (Law No. 29571).

11. Our ethical commitment

WashTrack is designed and built following the ACM/IEEE-CS Software Engineering Code of Ethics and Professional Practice and the Code of Ethics of the Colegio de Ingenieros del Perú (CIP). In practice, this means that we:

  • Public: act in the public interest and protect the privacy and safety of users.
  • Client and employer: act in the best interest of laundries and customers, without hiding risks or limitations.
  • Product: apply professional standards and test every version before publishing it.
  • Judgment: maintain integrity and independence in our technical decisions.
  • Management: plan and manage the work responsibly and transparently.
  • Profession: communicate honestly and never make misleading claims about the product.

1. Sobre WashTrack y estos términos

WashTrack es una plataforma digital desarrollada por DevTech, un equipo de estudiantes de Ingeniería de Software de la Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas (UPC), como parte de un proyecto académico. WashTrack conecta a las lavanderías con sus clientes para gestionar pedidos, hacer seguimiento de prendas, coordinar recojos y entregas, procesar pagos y monitorear las lavadoras mediante sensores IoT.

Estos Términos y Condiciones aplican a la landing page y a la aplicación web de WashTrack. Al utilizarlas, aceptas estos términos. Si no estás de acuerdo, te pedimos no utilizar el servicio.

2. Cuentas y roles

  • Administrador de lavandería: dueño o personal de una lavandería que gestiona clientes, pedidos, servicios, pagos y lavadoras.
  • Cliente: persona que solicita un servicio de lavandería y hace seguimiento al estado de su pedido.

Debes brindar información veraz, mantener la confidencialidad de tus credenciales y avisarnos de cualquier uso no autorizado de tu cuenta. Los menores de 18 años deben contar con el consentimiento de sus padres o tutores.

3. Planes, suscripciones y pagos

  • Las lavanderías pueden elegir el plan Gratuito (S/ 0), el plan Básico (S/ 49 al mes) o el plan Premium (S/ 99 al mes). Los precios están expresados en soles (PEN).

4. Responsabilidades de las lavanderías

Cada lavandería es responsable del servicio de lavado que brinda, de publicar precios y tiempos estimados correctos, de actualizar con veracidad el estado de cada pedido y del cuidado de las prendas que recibe. WashTrack es una herramienta de gestión y no presta directamente servicios de lavandería.

5. Responsabilidades de los clientes

Los clientes deben describir correctamente sus prendas y cuidados especiales, indicar una dirección válida para recojos y entregas, y pagar los servicios que solicitan.

6. Monitoreo IoT

Los datos de los sensores de las lavadoras (ciclos de uso, estado de funcionamiento y alertas) se utilizan únicamente para generar alertas de mantenimiento y métricas operativas para la lavandería. Las predicciones son estimaciones de apoyo a la toma de decisiones y no reemplazan el mantenimiento técnico recomendado por el fabricante.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

At WashTrack we process personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law of Peru (Law No. 29733) and its regulations.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact form: full name, email, user type, services of interest, and your message.
  • Account: username, email, and role (laundry administrator or customer).
  • Operation: orders, garments, pickup and delivery addresses, and payment status.
  • IoT: washer usage cycles and operating status. This data does not identify people.
  • Preferences: your language choice, saved only in your own browser.

The landing page does not use advertising cookies. Videos are embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode; if you play them, YouTube applies its own privacy policy.

2. Why we use your data

To provide the service, answer your inquiries, send notifications about your orders, keep the platform secure, and improve it. We never sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

Only with the laundry that handles your order, with the technology providers needed to operate the service (hosting and payment processing), and with authorities when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

While your account is active or for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Contact form inquiries are kept for up to 12 months.

5. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access, and the principle of least privilege to protect your information.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation, and opposition (ARCO) at any time through our contact form. We will answer within the time frames set by law.

7. Changes to this policy

We will publish any change to this policy on this page, indicating the date of the last update.
